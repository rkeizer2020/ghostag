Time Blender Demo

Run, jump and swing a golden sword through fifteen factory rooms. Every room has new platforms, lowered floor and three kinds of factory robots. The camera follows you; run off either end to reach the next room.

Keys: ← → or A D to run, Space ↑ or W to jump, Q to swing the sword, S or ↓ to drop through a platform, H to take damage, J to heal, [ ] to change room. Click the game first so it can read your keys.
